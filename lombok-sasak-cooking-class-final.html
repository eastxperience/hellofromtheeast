Questions, answered

The things every traveller asks.

Do I need any cooking experience?
None at all. The class is designed for all levels and complete beginners — your local hosts guide you through every step, from grinding spices to plating up.
Where is it, and can you pick me up?
It takes place in Bonjeruk Tourism Village, Central Lombok. Optional hotel pickup and drop-off is available for selected areas — share your hotel when you book and we'll confirm. Please be ready in the lobby ~10 minutes before pickup.
How long does it take?
About 4 hours, including the herbal welcome, the hands-on cooking session, and the village lunch you prepare together.
What will we cook? Vegetarian / allergies?
Traditional Sasak dishes such as Nasi Goreng Sasak, Sate Kuncung, Pepes Jamur, Urap Timun, Tempe Mendhoan and Pisang Goreng. The menu shifts with seasonal ingredients. Tell us about allergies or dietary needs in advance and we'll adapt where we can.
What's included?
A local cooking instructor, a traditional herbal welcome drink, all ingredients and equipment, mineral water, recipe information, the lunch you cook, and a village contribution. Transport to/from the village, tips and travel insurance aren't included.
What should I bring & know?
Bring a camera and comfortable clothes. It's a traditional village setting with simple, authentic facilities (not fully wheelchair accessible due to uneven surfaces). Children are welcome with an adult. Please don't smoke indoors.
