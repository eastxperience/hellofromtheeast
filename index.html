N°04 — The flagship

Komodo Sailing 3D2N, aboard the Kanha Fleet.

Three days inside a national park that behaves like another planet — sailed slowly, so the famous moments happen when the crowds aren't there.

  • Day 1
    Embark & first sailBoard in Labuan Bajo, lunch under way, first snorkel at Kanawa or Taka Makassar's sandbar.
  • Dusk
    Kalong sunsetThousands of flying foxes cross an amber sky — watched from the bow, coffee or coconut in hand.
  • Day 2
    Padar sunriseThe ridge climb before dawn. Three bays, three colors of sand, and the light you came for.
  • Morning
    Pink Beach & the dragonsSwim off rose-tinted sand, then walk with a ranger among Komodo dragons on their own island.
  • Day 3
    Manta channelsDrift the current where mantas glide below — then a slow sail home through the islands.
  • Nights
    Liveaboard lifeMeals cooked on board, a sky without light pollution, and the sound of water against a wooden hull.

N°11 — Good questions

Asked before booking, answered honestly.

Is EastXperience a local operator?
Yes. EastXperience is operated by PT Salam Dari Timur, an Indonesian company with real operations in Labuan Bajo, Flores, Bandung, Bali, Lombok and Banggai. We are the operator, guide network and concierge behind your journey — not a reseller.
Why should I book direct instead of through an OTA?
Booking direct gives you faster answers, real-time availability, clearer cabin and room options, flexible customization and direct WhatsApp access to the team that actually runs your trip — with transparent local fees and no platform queue. Platforms are fine for browsing; direct is where the journey gets personal.
Is payment secure?
Yes. You receive a written itinerary and transparent quote before any payment. We confirm everything clearly in writing, use secure payment channels, and explain cancellation terms before you commit.
Do you handle Komodo National Park fees?
Yes, we arrange all park permits. Depending on package and nationality the entrance fee may be separate: for a 3D2N trip, estimate around IDR 650,000 per foreign guest and IDR 350,000 per Indonesian guest. Your concierge confirms the exact figure for your dates before you book.
Can you arrange private cabins?
Yes. Our liveaboards offer sharing and private cabins. Private cabin upgrades are subject to availability, and cabins are limited in peak season (July–September) — the earlier you ask, the more choice you have.
Can you help families?
Absolutely. We adjust pace, boats, treks and meals for children and older travelers — and we'll tell you honestly which routes suit your family and which don't.
Can you arrange halal-friendly trips?
Yes. Halal-friendly meals, prayer time in the routing and appropriate stops can be arranged across all our journeys — just mention it in your first message.
Can you handle corporate groups?
Yes. We run corporate and incentive programs end to end: group logistics, private transport, gala dinners, documentation, local guides, halal-friendly catering and custom routing across Indonesia. Proposals usually within 48 hours.
How fast do you reply?
On WhatsApp, usually under 5 minutes during Indonesian daytime hours — and always within a few hours, wherever you're writing from.
What if my dates are in peak season?
July–September departures, boat cabins and Komodo National Park quotas are genuinely limited. Send your dates as early as you can; we'll check real availability before you commit to anything, and suggest alternatives if your first choice is full.
Can you customize trips?
Yes — customization is the core of what we do. Tell us your dates, group and travel style, and we design the route around you: combining Komodo, Waerebo, Flores, Banggai, Java, Bali and beyond in one seamless journey.
Do you speak English?
Yes, our concierge team and guides speak English fluently.
Can you provide Mandarin or other language guides?
Yes — Mandarin-speaking guides and other languages can be arranged on request, subject to availability. Mention it when you send your dates so we can secure the right guide early.
