How the partnership works

From first hello to first commission — five steps.

  1. Register your agency.One WhatsApp or email with your agency name, country and website. We reply within one working day.
  2. Sign the partner agreement.Simple two-page agreement: commission tier, payment terms, cancellation policy. You receive your agent code.
  3. Receive the rate sheet & media kit.Net rates in one consistent Excel format, plus photo/video assets cleared for your marketing.
  4. Book with your agent code.Send bookings via the trade WhatsApp line or email. Written confirmation with rooming/cabin allocation within 24 hours.
  5. Earn on every departure.Commission settled per booking or monthly — your choice. Transparent statements, no chasing.
