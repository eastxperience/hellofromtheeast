Questions, answered

The things every traveller asks.

Are whale shark sightings guaranteed?
Whale sharks are wild animals. In Saleh Bay they gather around the fishermen's platforms and sightings are very high throughout the season — but no responsible operator can promise 100%. We depart at dawn for the best chance and advise the best months when you book.
Do I need to dive or be a strong swimmer?
No diving certification is needed — you snorkel at the surface with a life vest and your guide alongside. Basic comfort in open water helps. We always keep a safe, respectful distance from the animals.
How do I get to Sumbawa?
Fly into Sumbawa Besar (SWQ), where we meet you at the airport. Flights aren't included — send us your arrival details and we'll align the whole schedule around them.
What's the price and minimum group size?
From IDR 5,400,000 per person, with a minimum of 4 guests. Travelling as a couple or want a private departure? Message us and we'll send a tailored quote.
What exactly is included?
Car for 3 days, 2 nights hotel, a private boat to the whale sharks, a private speedboat to Moyo Island, breakfast at sea, snorkeling equipment, all entrance fees, and underwater GoPro documentation. Flights, personal expenses, tips and travel insurance aren't included.
What should I bring, and is it family-friendly?
Bring swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a light jacket for the pre-dawn boat, and a sense of adventure — it's a wild, early-start trip. It suits confident swimmers of most ages; tell us if you're travelling with children so we can plan around them.
