Why this experience matters

Not a viewpoint. A rewrite of history.

Questions, answered

The things every traveller asks.

How long is the day, and how far is the drive?
It's a full day. Gunung Padang is in Cianjur, in the West Java highlands a few hours from Bandung, so we pick up early (around 07:00) and return by early evening. Travel is in a comfortable AC vehicle with welcome snacks for the road.
How fit do I need to be?
A moderate level of fitness. The site is terraced with stone steps and some uneven ground. Wear comfortable, modest clothing and sturdy shoes, and bring a hat and sunscreen. The tour isn't suitable for guests with altitude sickness or babies under 1 year.
Is lunch included? Vegetarian / halal options?
Yes. A Sundanese village lunch is included, with vegetarian options and halal on request — just tell us when you book. Welcome snacks and mineral water are included throughout.
Is it really older than the pyramids?
Researchers estimate the oldest layers of Gunung Padang at more than 12,000 years — older than Stonehenge and the Egyptian pyramids. The site featured in Netflix's Ancient Apocalypse, and your guide shares the leading theories on-site.
What's the weather like, and what should I bring?
The tour runs rain or shine. Highland air can be cool, and the wet season (Nov–Mar) brings showers — pack a light rain jacket or umbrella, plus comfortable shoes, a hat and sunscreen.
Can children join? What's the cancellation policy?
Children are welcome — under 6 may join free with an accompanying adult. Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before departure. We confirm every detail with you on WhatsApp before anything is locked in.
